FAQ

Is this investment advice?

No. It's analytics. Rankings describe what has already moved.

How often does it refresh?

Every minute during market hours (09:15–15:30 IST); last close otherwise.

Why is a stock missing?

Choppy stocks score zero and are hidden from the boards.

Which data does it use?

Yahoo Finance, delayed about 15 minutes. The terminal refreshes every 10 seconds while the market is open, and falls back to simulated prices if the feed is down.

About

ZoneDesk is a demo terminal for intraday F&O traders. It shows which stocks are moving, in which direction, and where the nearest order block and liquidity sit.

It is analytics software. It gives no tips, manages no money and places no trades.
